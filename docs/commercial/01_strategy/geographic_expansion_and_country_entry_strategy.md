# Sitolo — Geographic Expansion & Country Entry Strategy

**Status:** Commercial expansion control document  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Base market:** Malawi  
**Expansion principle:** evidence before geography

---

## 1. Principle

Sitolo is designed for African SMEs.

That does not mean launching across Africa simultaneously.

```MALAWI
→ PROVE
→ DOCUMENT
→ REPEAT
→ ADAPT
→ ENTER NEXT COUNTRY
```

---

## 2. Why Country Expansion Is Not Copy/Paste

Each country can differ in:

- payments;
- tax;
- data protection;
- consumer rules;
- telecom;
- currency;
- language;
- device economics;
- connectivity;
- distribution;
- business registration.

---

## 3. Malawi Exit Criteria

Before geographic expansion, prove:

- product activation;
- paid conversion;
- retention;
- positive segment contribution;
- repeatable acquisition;
- support capacity;
- payment collection;
- regulatory operating model;
- operational recovery.

---

## 4. Country Entry Dossier

Every candidate country requires:

### Market

- SME structure;
- target segments;
- competitor set;
- pricing;
- digital adoption;
- distribution channels.

### Payments

- major rails;
- provider options;
- licensing;
- settlement;
- fees;
- refunds/disputes.

### Fiscal

- VAT/tax;
- invoice rules;
- fiscal device/e-invoicing;
- tax integrations.

### Privacy

- data protection law;
- controller/processor obligations;
- registration;
- cross-border transfer.

### Operations

- support;
- device constraints;
- network;
- local staffing;
- language.

---

## 5. Country Risk Scorecard

Do not use a single “market attractiveness” score.

Instead record factual dimensions:

- addressable target segment;
- payment availability;
- compliance complexity;
- acquisition cost;
- support cost;
- localization effort;
- integration effort;
- infrastructure risk.

Management decides from the documented evidence.

---

## 6. Localization Layers

```
CORE PLATFORM
+
COUNTRY CONFIGURATION
+
LOCAL TAX
+
LOCAL PAYMENTS
+
LOCAL LANGUAGE
+
LOCAL DISTRIBUTION
```

Avoid forking the whole platform.

---

## 7. Currency

The core domain must support country-specific currency configuration and preserve exact monetary semantics.

Historical transactions keep their original currency/state.

---

## 8. Regulatory Localization

Never assume Malawi approvals transfer to another country.

Each new country needs independent legal/provider validation.

---

## 9. Payment Localization

Provider adapters should allow:

```
COUNTRY
→ AUTHORIZED PAYMENT PROVIDER(S)
→ NORMALIZED PAYMENT DOMAIN
```

Do not leak provider semantics into common business logic.

---

## 10. Tax Localization

Tax is:

```
COUNTRY RULESET
+
EFFECTIVE DATE
+
PRODUCT/TAX CONFIGURATION
```

Keep historical transaction snapshots immutable.

---

## 11. Distribution Localization

Test:

- accountant channel;
- distributor channel;
- agent channel;
- reseller channel;
- direct digital.

Do not transplant the Malawi channel model without evidence.

---

## 12. Support Localization

Consider:

- language;
- hours;
- field coverage;
- payment support;
- local partner escalation.

---

## 13. Geographic Rollout Shape

Prefer:

```
PILOT CLUSTER
→ PROOF
→ ADJACENT CLUSTER
→ NATIONAL COVERAGE
```

This reduces operational blast radius.

---

## 14. Exit / Pause Criteria

Pause expansion if:

- compliance uncertain;
- payment rail unreliable;
- CAC unproven;
- support cost uncontrolled;
- activation poor;
- localization creates excessive complexity;
- contribution negative without a deliberate investment case.

---

## 15. Country Entry Economics

```
SETUP COST
+
LOCALIZATION
+
REGULATORY
+
INTEGRATION
+
SUPPORT
+
ACQUISITION
=
COUNTRY ENTRY INVESTMENT
```

Compare against expected validated contribution, not raw market size.

---

## 16. Expansion Dependency

A country should not depend on undocumented knowledge held by one founder or engineer.

Store:

- source evidence;
- provider contracts;
- configuration;
- runbooks;
- partner terms;
- lessons learned.

---

## 17. Final Contract

Africa is the product horizon.

Malawi is the evidence environment.

Expansion happens after the commercial and operational machine is repeatable.
