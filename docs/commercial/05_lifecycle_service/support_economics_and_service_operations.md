# Sitolo — Support Economics & Service Operations

**Status:** Commercial operating-model control document  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Scope:** Duka, SME, Growth, Multi-Branch, Enterprise

---

## 1. Purpose

Support is a variable economic cost and a retention mechanism.

The target is not “zero tickets.”

The target is:

```HIGH FIRST-VALUE
+
LOW AVOIDABLE SUPPORT
+
FAST SAFE RESOLUTION
```

---

## 2. Support Tiers

| Tier | Model |
|---|---|
| Duka | self-service + lightweight escalation |
| SME | standard support |
| Growth | standard / priority |
| Multi-Branch | priority |
| Enterprise | contractual SLA / dedicated path |

Actual entitlements are hypotheses until validated.

---

## 3. Support Channels

Potential:

- in-product help;
- knowledge base;
- messaging;
- email;
- voice;
- partner escalation;
- field support.

Use high-cost channels selectively.

---

## 4. Support Cost Model

```SUPPORT COST
=
AGENT TIME
+
CHANNEL COST
+
TRAVEL
+
TRAINING
+
PARTNER SUPPORT
+
ESCALATIONS
```

Measure direct variable cost per active account.

---

## 5. Duka Economics

A MK7,500/month plan cannot sustainably absorb enterprise-style support.

Therefore:

- onboarding must be lightweight;
- common issues must be self-service;
- product errors must be instrumented;
- field assistance must be limited;
- partner support must be carefully priced.

---

## 6. Enterprise Economics

Enterprise may require:

- onboarding specialists;
- implementation;
- support SLA;
- account management;
- integration assistance.

Those costs belong in enterprise contribution calculations.

---

## 7. Support Taxonomy

Classify contacts:

- payment;
- billing;
- access;
- device;
- offline/sync;
- stock;
- sale;
- reconciliation;
- tax/EIS;
- export;
- permissions;
- training;
- suspected fraud;
- defect.

This creates product-feedback loops.

---

## 8. Severity

Use at least:

### S0 — Critical

Material business-wide inability, integrity risk or security emergency.

### S1 — High

Major business workflow blocked.

### S2 — Normal

Significant issue with workaround.

### S3 — Low

Question, request or cosmetic issue.

Exact response targets belong in the service-level design.

---

## 9. Support vs Incident

A customer question is not necessarily an incident.

Security/integrity failures must enter the incident process.

Do not hide:

- tenant isolation failures;
- unauthorized access;
- financial corruption;
- data loss;

inside normal ticket queues.

---

## 10. First Contact Resolution

Track:

- first-contact resolution;
- repeat contacts;
- escalations;
- transfer count;
- reopen rate.

High repeat contact often indicates a product defect or poor documentation.

---

## 11. Self-Service

Prioritize self-service for:

- password/account recovery within approved flow;
- device replacement;
- basic product setup;
- billing FAQs;
- plan information;
- standard reports;
- export initiation where allowed.

Do not self-service high-risk authorization bypasses.

---

## 12. Agent / Partner Support

Partners may handle:

- setup;
- basic training;
- first-line troubleshooting.

Sitolo retains control over:

- security;
- tenant access;
- financial corrections;
- high-risk recovery;
- regulated integrations.

---

## 13. Support Economics by Segment

```DUKA
LOW ARPU → LOW TOUCH

SME
MID ARPU → STANDARD TOUCH

GROWTH
HIGHER VALUE → MORE ASSISTED

MULTI-BRANCH
COMPLEXITY → PRIORITY

ENTERPRISE
HIGH ACV → CONTRACTUAL SERVICE
```

The support model follows contribution capacity.

---

## 14. Product Feedback Loop

```SUPPORT CONTACT
→ ROOT CAUSE
→ DEFECT / UX / EDUCATION / POLICY
→ FIX
→ MEASURE CONTACT REDUCTION
```

Support should not become a permanent substitute for fixing repetitive product failures.

---

## 15. Support Metrics

Per segment:

- contacts/account;
- cost/contact;
- cost/account;
- first response;
- resolution;
- first-contact resolution;
- repeat contact;
- escalation;
- support-caused churn.

---

## 16. Commercial Risk

Support can destroy contribution when:

- onboarding is manual;
- pricing is too low;
- documentation is weak;
- devices vary widely;
- connectivity failures are frequent;
- partners are poorly trained.

Monitor support cost before scaling volume.

---

## 17. Trust-Sensitive Support

Special treatment is required for:

- missing data;
- disputed money;
- unauthorized changes;
- security incidents;
- lost devices;
- exports;
- account recovery.

The response must be evidence-based.

---

## 18. Support Capacity

Plan capacity from:

```
ACTIVE ACCOUNTS
×
CONTACT RATE
×
AVERAGE HANDLE TIME
```

Do not hire only after a queue becomes operationally unsafe.

---

## 19. Final Support Contract

Support is successful when the merchant:

1. gets operational safely;
2. trusts the result;
3. does not require unnecessary human intervention;
4. receives a durable fix when the problem is systemic.
