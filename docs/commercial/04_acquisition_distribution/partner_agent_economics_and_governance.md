# Sitolo — Partner, Agent & Reseller Economics and Governance

**Status:** Commercial operating-model control document  
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Market:** Malawi first  
**Scope:** acquisition, onboarding, support, referrals, assisted collection and future ecosystem partnerships

---

## 1. Purpose

Partners can reduce Sitolo's distribution cost and increase trust, but they can also create:

- commission leakage;
- fraud;
- channel conflict;
- support cost;
- customer-data exposure;
- operational dependency;
- reputational risk.

The partner model must therefore be an economic system with security and governance, not an informal referral network.

---

## 2. Partner Types

| Type | Core role | May acquire | May onboard | May support | May collect money |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Referral partner | introduction | yes | no/limited | no | no |
| Accountant/bookkeeper | trusted influence | yes | possible | possible | no |
| Distributor/wholesaler | merchant reach | yes | possible | limited | only under separate approved arrangement |
| Technology agent | field assistance | yes | yes | yes | no |
| Reseller | commercial sale | yes | yes | yes | only where explicitly authorized |
| Payment partner | collection rail | indirect | no | provider support | through authorized rail |

The commercial contract must specify the exact role.

---

## 3. Partner Identity

Every partner should have:

- legal/business identity;
- named contacts;
- territory where relevant;
- contract status;
- commercial terms;
- channel ID;
- commission plan;
- security obligations;
- support level;
- start/end dates;
- status.

Partner status should include:

```text
PROSPECT
→ APPROVED
→ ACTIVE
→ SUSPENDED
→ TERMINATED
```

---

## 4. Commission Principles

Commission should normally be tied to verified economic outcomes.

Prefer:

```PAID + RETAINED ACCOUNT
```

over:

```RAW LEAD
```

Possible commission events:

- first paid subscription;
- retained 30/90-day subscription;
- expansion;
- implementation fee.

Avoid paying large commissions before the account demonstrates real value.

---

## 5. Commission Formula

```PARTNER CONTRIBUTION
=
ATTRIBUTED NET REVENUE
−
COMMISSION
−
INCENTIVES
−
TRAINING
−
SUPPORT
−
TRAVEL
−
FRAUD / DISPUTE COST
```

For low-ARPU Duka accounts, partner commission must be compatible with the entire CAC payback requirement.

---

## 6. Territory Model

A partner may have:

- nationwide rights;
- regional rights;
- district rights;
- non-exclusive territory;
- named-account rights.

Exclusive territory should require evidence of delivery capacity.

Do not grant exclusivity merely because a partner asks for it.

---

## 7. Channel Conflict Rules

The commercial ledger should support:

- source attribution;
- assist attribution where needed;
- partner override with evidence;
- customer-owned referral codes;
- acquisition timestamp.

A customer should not generate multiple full commissions merely because several parties interacted with them.

---

## 8. Partner Onboarding

Minimum controls:

1. verify partner identity;
2. approve role;
3. execute terms;
4. issue least-privilege access where required;
5. train;
6. assign attribution mechanism;
7. activate;
8. monitor.

No production access before the relevant controls exist.

---

## 9. Data Boundary

Partner access must be limited to the minimum required.

Examples:

- referral partner: no customer business data;
- onboarding agent: only permitted support/setup scope;
- accountant: only organization-authorized data;
- reseller: commercial/customer support metadata within contractual limits.

The organization remains the owner of its business records.

---

## 10. Agent Support Model

Field agents can reduce onboarding friction.

Potential activities:

- install application;
- configure basic settings;
- enter opening inventory;
- demonstrate first sale;
- explain support path.

Agents must not:

- bypass authentication;
- access another merchant;
- export data without authorization;
- alter financial history;
- create hidden administrative users.

---

## 11. Assisted Payment Boundary

An agent collecting money on behalf of Sitolo is a materially different role from an agent selling software.

If used:

- define legal basis;
- use auditable collection identity;
- reconcile every receipt;
- prevent duplicate collection;
- settle on a fixed process;
- define dispute handling;
- monitor cash exposure.

The preferred model is provider/authorized-rail collection rather than informal cash custody.

---

## 12. Partner Fraud Controls

Monitor:

- fake referrals;
- self-referrals;
- duplicate businesses;
- churn/rejoin gaming;
- fake payments;
- unauthorized discounts;
- data access anomalies;
- suspicious account creation patterns;
- commission anomalies.

High-risk events should trigger investigation and possible suspension.

---

## 13. Partner Support Economics

Measure support by partner.

A partner that creates many customers but requires intensive Sitolo support may be uneconomic.

Track:

- tickets/account;
- training hours;
- escalations;
- travel;
- time-to-resolution;
- product issues attributable to onboarding.

---

## 14. Partner Scorecard

Monthly:

| Metric | Required |
|---|---|
| qualified leads | yes |
| paid accounts | yes |
| activation | yes |
| 90-day retention | yes |
| contribution | yes |
| commission | yes |
| support cost | yes |
| fraud/dispute rate | yes |
| concentration share | yes |

No partner should be judged on acquisition count alone.

---

## 15. Reseller Commercial Contract

A reseller agreement should define:

- price authority;
- discount authority;
- invoicing;
- customer ownership;
- support;
- branding;
- marketing claims;
- commission;
- data handling;
- termination;
- audit rights;
- fraud handling.

Resellers must not invent regulatory, security or tax claims.

---

## 16. Distributor Integration Strategy

A distributor may introduce Sitolo where merchants already gather for purchasing.

Validate:

- introduction rate;
- trial start;
- first sale;
- payment conversion;
- retention;
- incremental sales to distributor;
- partner workload.

Do not force integration into distributor systems before the commercial relationship proves valuable.

---

## 17. Accountant Partnership

Accountants may become a high-trust commercial channel.

Possible benefit:

- merchant acquisition;
- bookkeeping workflow;
- tax-readiness awareness;
- monthly business reviews.

Any accountant access to Sitolo data must be explicitly authorized by the customer organization.

---

## 18. Device Partner Economics

Future device partners may bundle:

```DEVICE
+
DATA
+
SITOLO
+
SUPPORT
```

The bundle must separately account for:

- hardware margin;
- software contribution;
- financing/subsidy;
- warranty;
- device replacement;
- support.

Do not subsidize hardware using Duka SaaS margin unless the whole bundle is profitable or explicitly funded.

---

## 19. Partner Concentration

Track:

```
TOP PARTNER % OF NEW ACCOUNTS
TOP PARTNER % OF REVENUE
TOP PARTNER % OF SUPPORT LOAD
```

A high concentration requires an exit/backup strategy.

---

## 20. Termination

A partner may be terminated for:

- fraud;
- security violation;
- misrepresentation;
- persistent poor activation;
- uneconomic delivery;
- repeated support failures;
- contract breach.

Termination must not orphan customer organizations.

Customer identity remains with Sitolo.

---

## 21. Final Partner Contract

```text
PARTNER REACH
→ VALID CUSTOMER
→ FIRST VALUE
→ PAID
→ RETAINED
→ CONTRIBUTIVE
```

The partner earns durable economics only when the customer does.
