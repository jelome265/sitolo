# Sitolo Commercial Workspace

Mode: routing-only
Primary owner: Market/segment strategy, validation, pricing/packaging, acquisition/distribution, monetization and commercial economics.

## Entry condition

Enter only after `workspaces/CLAUDE.md` selects Commercial as the primary work domain. Strategy remains inside the commercial strategy layer; finance and payments remain cross-domain concerns unless future authoritative ownership evidence establishes otherwise.

## Workflow destination

Primary destination: `CONTEXT.md` for the smallest commercial evidence set and resulting commercial decision, experiment, model or analysis artifact. Lifecycle, regulated and technical consequences exit through Cross-domain exits.

## Cross-domain exits

| Dependency | Destination |
|---|---|
| Retention, onboarding, support or service operations | ../sitolo-customer-operations/CONTEXT.md |
| Regulatory or payments boundary | ../sitolo-trust-compliance/CONTEXT.md |
| Technical implementation | ../sitolo-engineering/CLAUDE.md |
| Product behavior/capability decision | ../sitolo-product/CONTEXT.md |

## Routing

| Task | Go To |
|---|---|
| Business model and segment strategy | CONTEXT.md |
| Pricing, packaging and buyer process | CONTEXT.md |
| Acquisition, channels and partners | CONTEXT.md |
| Retention, lifecycle and support economics | ../sitolo-customer-operations/CONTEXT.md |
| Regulatory/payment boundary | ../sitolo-trust-compliance/CONTEXT.md |
| Technical implementation | ../sitolo-engineering/CLAUDE.md |

## Loading

Read CONTEXT.md, then only the relevant commercial source groups.

## Authority

Commercial assumptions remain hypotheses until the governing validation corpus records evidence.

## Do NOT load

Do not load engineering implementation details, full regulatory text or the entire commercial corpus for a narrow commercial decision.
