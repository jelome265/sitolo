## Process

1. Identify the commercial question and segment.
2. Classify each claim as hypothesis, observed evidence or approved decision.
3. Load the smallest source set that answers the question.
4. Keep strategy, validation, pricing, acquisition and lifecycle concerns explicit rather than blending them.
5. Route regulated or technical consequences to the appropriate workspace.

## Output

Commercial decision, experiment, model, analysis or operating artifact.

## Do NOT load

Do not load engineering implementation details, full regulatory text or the entire commercial corpus for a narrow commercial decision.
