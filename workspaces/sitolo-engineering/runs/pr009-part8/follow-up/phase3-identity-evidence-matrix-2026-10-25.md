## Phase 3 disposition

Phase 3 is **not complete**. Existing token/session/MFA/device unit state machines are useful local evidence, and the external identity mapping/session acceptance path has a real PostgreSQL test. They do not establish durable refresh, MFA, recovery and device lifecycles, real provider end-to-end behavior, comprehensive disabled-user invalidation, or secret-free production logging. Complete these items on the binding Phase 3 contract before claiming Phase 3 closure. No owner decision or production provider credentials are inferred from fixture tests.
