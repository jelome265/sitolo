# Sitolo Customer Operations Context

## Inputs

| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding and migration | ../../docs/commercial/05_lifecycle_service/onboarding_and_migration_strategy.md | Full file when required | Activation and migration |
| Retention/lifecycle | ../../docs/commercial/05_lifecycle_service/retention_churn_and_customer_lifecycle.md | Relevant sections | Lifecycle and churn taxonomy |
| Support operations | ../../docs/commercial/05_lifecycle_service/support_economics_and_service_operations.md | Full file when required | Support model, severity and cost |

| Data governance | ../../docs/commercial/05_lifecycle_service/data_portability_and_data_governance.md | Relevant sections | Portability and operational governance |

## Process

1. Identify the lifecycle event or service operation.
2. Determine the customer segment and actor affected.
3. Load only the applicable service source.
4. Separate operational practice from product requirements and commercial assumptions.
5. Escalate integrity/security/regulatory events to Trust & Compliance and technical changes to Engineering.

## Output

Onboarding, support, lifecycle, service-operation or customer-continuity artifact.

## Do NOT load

Do not load the entire commercial corpus or technical repository for a customer-operations task.
