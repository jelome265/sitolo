# Sitolo Customer Operations Workspace

Mode: routing-only
Primary owner: Onboarding, migration, support, service operations, lifecycle and operational customer continuity.

## Entry condition

Enter only after `workspaces/CLAUDE.md` selects Customer Operations as the primary work domain. Ownership covers how customers are onboarded, supported and operated through their lifecycle.

## Workflow destination

Primary destination: `CONTEXT.md` for the applicable lifecycle/service evidence and resulting onboarding, support, lifecycle, service-operation or continuity artifact. Product, commercial, technical and regulated consequences exit through Cross-domain exits.

## Cross-domain exits

| Dependency | Destination |
|---|---|
| Product requirement or experience change | ../sitolo-product/CONTEXT.md |
| Commercial economics or lifecycle model | ../sitolo-commercial/CONTEXT.md |
| Technical implementation | ../sitolo-engineering/CLAUDE.md |
| Regulated or security incident/control | ../sitolo-trust-compliance/CONTEXT.md |

Customer-operations artifacts do not redefine product, financial, security or regulatory authority.
