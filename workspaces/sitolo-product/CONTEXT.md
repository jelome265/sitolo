# Sitolo Product Context

## Inputs

| Source | File/Location | Scope | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product specification | ../../docs/sitolo.md | Full file when defining product scope | Product surface and invariants |
| Business model | ../../docs/commercial/01_strategy/business_model_design.md | Product scope, value, segments and product surfaces | Product/business boundary |
| Segment strategy | ../../docs/commercial/01_strategy/segment_strategy_and_duka_economics.md | Relevant segment/product sections | Progressive complexity and device doctrine |
| Onboarding | ../../docs/commercial/05_lifecycle_service/onboarding_and_migration_strategy.md | Relevant first-value/product sections | Activation and UX constraints |
| Intelligence | ../../docs/commercial/08_intelligence_defensibility/business_intelligence_alerts_and_action_model.md | Relevant insight/action sections | Product intelligence layer |
| Validation | ../../docs/commercial/02_validation/commercial_validation_plan.md | Experiment/product-demand sections | Evidence vs hypothesis |

## Process

1. Establish the product decision and affected actor.
2. Load only the relevant canonical source sections.
3. Separate product requirements from commercial hypotheses and implementation constraints.
4. Record unresolved product decisions explicitly.
5. Route technical consequences to Engineering and validation/commercial consequences to the appropriate workspace.

## Output

Product decision, requirement, hypothesis, or product research artifact.

## Do NOT load

Do not load the full technical architecture, database design, or entire commercial corpus unless the decision explicitly depends on them.
